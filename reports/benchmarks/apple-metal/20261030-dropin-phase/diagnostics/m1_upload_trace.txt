M1 warm-upload trace (diagnostic only, untimed, not a verdict), 2026-10-31, apple-host.
Image workload (4 MiB wgpuQueueWriteBuffer). Each row: per-process mean of the
instrumented interval over iterations 3..96, median over 12-14 interleaved
processes under the GPU lock, ratio against main in the same run.

1. Split of wgpuQueueWriteBuffer (buffers.c:415-454 instrumented at lock, settle,
   write, unlock, release): only the write (validate + CPU memmove into the shared
   allocation, resources.c:79-97) differs. main 116.5 us, M1 120.3 us. Lock,
   settle_for_buffer and unlock are equal (<0.1 us). The trailing device release
   costs about 1 us on main and M1 (worker wake) and 0.02 us with the completion
   change.
2. Pre-reading the caller's source array before the memmove: source read main
   54.8 us vs M1 56.8 us (+3.6%); the memmove from the warmed source is equal
   (about 62.3 us both). The extra time is the first read of the application's
   input array, not Doe's copy.
3. Variants that did not remove it (ratio to main): M1 1.031-1.064; flush the
   upload destination at map 1.040; flush the lent range at unmap 1.038; Doe-side
   read of the lent range at map 1.040; write a 4 MiB scratch at map 1.064; read
   and write 1.058; malloc+memset+free 1.053; copy into a malloc shadow and free
   at unmap while still lending 1.057; spin 130 us at map 1.032 or at unmap 1.021
   (rules out DVFS and time-since-completion); completion handler change plus M1
   1.044 (rules out the worker wake).
4. main plus an extra CPU read of the readback buffer at unmap 1.006; main plus an
   extra read of the shadow at unmap 0.976.
5. m1_upload_probe.m reproduces the buffer sizes and order without WebGPU (blit
   or compute pass, lend vs malloc shadow): no difference (lend 57.4-61.8 us,
   shadow 59.1-62.0 us source read).

Reading: the regression is in the CPU's first read of the caller-owned source
array, it depends on whether the harness's own memcpy read the shared readback
allocation or a malloc shadow, and it is not reproduced outside Doe. No Doe-side
cost in the upload path was found, so no fix was made and no revised M1 contract
was frozen. In m1r the 1 MiB workloads moved the other way (upload 0.953, 0.934).
