# Retained benchmark evidence

Inherit repository instructions and [report retention](../README.md#retention).
`sources.json` registers retained bytes; it does not copy or edit measurements.

CURRENT.md, index.json and families/*.md are generated by the existing cube.
Change [the inventory owner](../../bench/inventory/AGENTS.md), then run
`python3 bench/cli.py inventory` and `python3 bench/cli.py inventory --check`.
Never hand-edit generated winners, dates, identities or qualification.

Dated evidence keeps its historical scope and checksum. Publication authority
belongs to ../claim-index.json, separately from inventory discovery.
