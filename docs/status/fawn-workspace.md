# Fawn durable workspace

Fawn provides durable browser workspaces for agents above stock
Chromium/CDP/Playwright; its shared observation, action, and recovery service is
the next build. [The contract](../fawn-workspace-contract.md)
owns authority, observation/action binding, recovery and acceptance.
`fawnWorkspace` in [the strategy projection](../../config/doe-product-strategy.json)
owns ordered steps; its assessments remain unestablished.

The shared service is **not implemented**. Missing boundaries are the trusted
dispatcher, durable authorization and owner generations, versioned observation
checks and uncertain-action journal. Document research, controlled form submission
and artifact export have not passed through this service. Behavioral fault,
handoff and competent-baseline evidence remains absent; workspace completion and
release claims are blocked.

The next implementation is shared durable identity/authority/action storage and
narrow adapters, followed by dispatch-time admission and serialized handoff under
the contract: persisted ownership generations, stale-command rejection, and
completed, failed, or unknown outcomes retained across restart. Strategy/schema
tests qualify the policy projection only.

Existing [Chromium runtime integration](../browser-lane.md) and
[hosted artwork](fawn-start.md) keep separate evidence. Their receipts do not
establish workspace authority or external-action recovery. Chromium hooks and
browser-runtime integration wait for measured benefits that justify their
compatibility and maintenance costs. Native compiler/runtime
work retains its own milestone IDs, numerical gates and platform admission.
