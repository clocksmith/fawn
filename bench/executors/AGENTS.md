# Measurement execution

Inherit [benchmark instructions](../AGENTS.md). Own one product's actual work,
timed intervals, output/trace capture and process status. Comparisons, qualification
and inventory presentation belong to their respective owners.

Capture loaded artifacts and completed work rather than requested settings. Keep
metadata outside timing and use shared provenance where its receipt contract applies.
Never make missing output, hidden fallback or a failed process look successful.
Run the affected executor's focused controls; ordinary edits do not launch GPUs.
